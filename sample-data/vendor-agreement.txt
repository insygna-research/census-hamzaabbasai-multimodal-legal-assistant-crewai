VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Vendor Services Agreement is made between Northstar Retail GmbH
("Customer") and BrightDesk Software Ltd ("Supplier").

1. SERVICES

Supplier will provide the hosted reporting service described in Schedule A.
The service will be available from 1 August 2026.

2. FEES

Customer will pay EUR 4,800 each month. Supplier may change the fees at its
sole discretion and at any time by sending an email to Customer.

3. TERM AND RENEWAL

The initial term is twelve months. This agreement will automatically renew for
another twelve-month term unless Customer gives written notice at least ninety
days before the renewal date.

4. DATA PROTECTION

Supplier may process personal data outside the European Economic Area without
additional contractual safeguards. Supplier will take reasonable steps to
protect account information.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party must protect confidential information and may only use it to perform
this agreement.

6. LIABILITY

Customer's liability under this agreement is unlimited, including liability
for indirect loss, lost profits, and claims made by third parties.

7. INDEMNITY

Customer shall indemnify Supplier, its directors, employees, and contractors
against all claims connected with Customer's use of the service.

8. TERMINATION

Supplier may terminate this agreement for convenience with five days of
written notice. Customer may terminate for a material breach that remains
unfixed for thirty days.

9. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the laws of Germany. The courts of Berlin have
exclusive jurisdiction.
